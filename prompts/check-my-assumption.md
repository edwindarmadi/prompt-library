# Check my assumption

**When to use:** You think you know what follows from a situation and want to check whether your understanding is right, and where it isn't.

## Prompt

```
Under the assumption that [certain criteria], I think [assumed outcome]. Is that correct? Tell me yes, no, or partly, explain why, and point out any assumption I'm missing.
```

**Original wording:** "So under the assumption that [certain criteria], [assumed outcome based off my understanding]"
