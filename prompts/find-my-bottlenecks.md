**When to use:** Send to an agent that already knows your work (your chats, tools, or routines) to find the tasks where you're slowing things down and that it or another AI agent could take over. Good for deciding what to delegate or automate next.

**Source:** @nateliason (Nat Eliason) on X (Nov 1 2026, 890 likes / 1,675 bookmarks).

## Prompt

```
Looking through everything I do, to the best of your knowledge, what are places where I’m a bottleneck that you or another AI agent could take over
```
