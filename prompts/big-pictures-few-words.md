# Big pictures, few words

**When to use:** You want an explanation, slide, or write-up that leads with large visuals and stays short on text — not dense walls of words.

**Source:** [Thariq (@trq212)](https://x.com/trq212/status/2102186805034635576) — “I now type ‘use big pictures and few words’ several times a day”
