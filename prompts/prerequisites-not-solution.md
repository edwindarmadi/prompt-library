# Prerequisites, not the solution

**When to use:** You want to solve something you don't fully understand. You want reminders of missing prerequisites — not the full answer.

## Prompt

```
Give me the understanding on how to solve this. Don't give me the solution. Just remind me of the pre-requisites needed to solve this
```
