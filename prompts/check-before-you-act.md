# Check before you act

**When to use:** Paste at the end of a fuzzy, multi-step, or hard-to-undo ask. Forces the agent to restate the goal and wait for your OK before doing any work. Skip on tiny one-shot asks.

**Note:** Evolved from Edwin’s short “Verify you understand what I mean / what my desired outcome is” habit.

## Prompt

```
Before you do anything:
1. Restate my goal in your own words.
2. List what you think “done” looks like.
3. List any assumptions you’re making, and anything that’s still unclear.
4. Stop. Wait for me to confirm or correct you. Do not start the work until I say to proceed.
```
