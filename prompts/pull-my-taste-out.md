**When to use:** Paste at the start of a task when you know what good looks like but can't fully spell it out. It hands the AI the job of asking questions and checking with you until it really understands your taste and vision, instead of guessing.

**Source:** @tanishqk (Tanishq) on X (Nov 2 2026, 179 likes / 218 bookmarks), shared as a screenshot captioned "trying something new". It quotes his earlier "exercise abstract thinking" post, which is already filed as [exercise-abstract-thinking](exercise-abstract-thinking.md).

## Prompt

```
See, there's a communication issue between you and I. I have taste - you have grit. I am unable to fully communicate my taste to you by myself. You are responsible for pulling my taste and vision out of me and aggressively ensure we're aligned.
```
