# Restate goals and problem

**When to use:** Before solving — make the model say back your goals and the problem so you can catch a mismatch early.

**Source:** [lauren (@poteto)](https://x.com/poteto/status/2104744961904394699) — “this has become one of my most used prompts recently”

## Prompt

```
restate in your own words what you think my goals are and what the problem i'm trying to solve is
```
