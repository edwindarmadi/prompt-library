# Invert the goal

**When to use:** Before committing to a plan — failure-first thinking without jargon. Forces the model to map how to cause the opposite of your goal, then turn each into something to avoid or watch for.

**Note:** Workshopped from the dig find “Apply Inversion to my plan…” (AI Maker / Substack). Kept Edwin’s clearer “invert the goal” wording.

## Prompt

```
If my goal is [goal], list the surest ways to make the opposite happen. Then flip each into one concrete thing I should avoid or watch for.
```
